# Linqto

> Linqto is a San Jose, California private-markets investment platform that gives individual accredited investors access to equity in mid-to-late-stage private technology companies before an IPO or acquisition. Linqto buys shares itself and delivers them to investors as units in a series-LLC private fund ("Liquidshares") holding the underlying company's stock, charging a single purchase premium with no carry, profit, legal, or administrative fees. The company filed for Chapter 11 protection in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas in 2025 and has paused transactions while continuing operations.

## API surface

Linqto publishes **no public developer API, no OpenAPI/Swagger specification, no GraphQL
endpoint, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, no SDK, and no developer portal.** Contract
discovery on 2026-08-01 probed every candidate spec path on www.linqto.com, app.linqto.com and
api.app.linqto.com; api.linqto.com, developer.linqto.com and docs.linqto.com do not resolve.
The investor application at app.linqto.com is served by a private, session-authenticated
backend at api.app.linqto.com that publishes no contract and is not an integration surface.

The only anonymously callable machine-readable surfaces are the WordPress infrastructure
behind the marketing site:

- [WordPress REST API](https://www.linqto.com/wp-json/wp/v2): standard WP REST API on the
  linqto.com site; 472 routes across 19 namespaces. Reference:
  https://developer.wordpress.org/rest-api/
